Privacy policy

DCR Card Vault helps collectors organize their DCR certified cards. This policy explains the information used to sign in and store a collection.

Information we store

When you sign in with Google, we store your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile photo URL. Your vault may store card names, sets, years, card numbers, grades, certificate numbers, status, prices, dates, purchase or sale sources, and notes that you enter.

How we use it

We use this information to create your account, keep you signed in, and show your saved cards, certificate search results, and labels. Card records are private to the account that created or imported them. We do not sell account or collection data.

Google sign-in

Google Identity Services is used to authenticate your account. DCR Card Vault requests the basic identity scopes for sign-in only. It does not access your Gmail inbox or email messages.

Storage and security

Account and card data are stored in Cloudflare D1. Sign-in uses an HttpOnly session cookie; the database stores a hash of the session token. Sessions expire after 30 days. Card photos are not uploaded or stored by the current app.

Importing old browser records

On first sign-in from a browser with older DCR Vault records, you can select which cards to import. The original browser copy remains in place. Import does not upload card photos.

Questions or data requests

For questions or requests to correct or remove account data, contact the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.
